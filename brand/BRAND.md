# Brand, for Titanium Bot Lite

Product: Titanium Bot. Maker: Titanium Computing (titaniumcomputing.com). The lite app is
"Brought to you by Titanium Bot" and links to https://titanium.bot.

Marks
- `ti-mark.svg`: the rounded Ti tile mark.
- `titanium-bot-logo.svg`: the wordmark with the mark, from the console.
- `favicon.svg`: the tab icon.
- `../assets/app-icon/titanium-bot-1024.png`: the App Store icon (1024 px).
- Titan, the blob mascot with one eye: `../assets/console/titan-mascot.js` and the kit in
  `../assets/mascot-kit/` (mascots.js, mascots.css, mascot-crew.js); 39 character sprites in
  `../assets/console/characters/`.

Colour (the product's own board)
- Midnight #090D14 (ground), Signal Cyan #00C8F0 (accent), and the console's tokens in
  `tokens.css` (teal-300 #7fe3dc, teal-500 #31b6b8 and the rest).
- The Titanium Computing palette for anything company-facing: #0D1117, #1A1F26, #C0C5CC,
  #0EA5E9, #FF8A3D; Cinzel for display, Inter for text.

Backgrounds: 17 plates with thumbnails in `../assets/console/backgrounds/` (Titan Nebula is the
console's default; Habitat and The Lab series). Pick two or three for the lite app; do not ship
all 3 MB.

Copy: plain words for a business owner; contractions fine; no em dashes; never the old upstream's
name.
