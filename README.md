# Titanium Bot Lite

A small, local assistant for the Tiiny AI Pocket Lab, brought to you by [Titanium Bot](https://titanium.bot).

Titan lives on your pocket device: chat, memories, skills, routines and voice, talking to the model
running on the Tiiny itself. No cloud account, no server, nothing leaves the device unless you paste
a key and say so.

Made by Titanium Computing. The full product, with a team of bots, mail, a browser and a computer of
its own, is at https://titanium.bot.

See `SPEC.md` for what this is and `docs/` for the pieces it borrows from Titanium Bot.
